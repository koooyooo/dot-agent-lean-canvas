    <td rowspan="2" colspan="2" class="lc-problem">
      <div class="lc-h">Problem</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>People want to watch videos on demand, but are constrained by broadcast schedules or physical rentals</li>
          <li>Creators want to publish their videos, but video hosting, encoding, and sharing are prohibitively heavy</li>
          <li>Advertisers want targeted exposure near engaged audiences without TV-level costs</li>
        </ul>
        <div class="lc-sub"><b>Existing Alternatives</b>
          <ul>
          <li>Broadcast TV, DVD rentals (Blockbuster, Netflix mail-order)</li>
          <li>Self-hosted servers, peer-to-peer file sharing, Google Video, Vimeo</li>
          <li>TV commercials, display banners, search ads</li>
        </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td colspan="2" class="lc-solution">
      <div class="lc-h">Solution</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>Instant in-browser streaming for any searched video</li>
          <li>Frictionless video upload and public hosting with a free account</li>
          <li>Contextual video ad placements with creator revenue sharing</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td rowspan="2" colspan="2" class="lc-uvp">
      <div class="lc-h">Unique Value Proposition</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>Find and watch any video you want, instantly</li>
          <li>Concept: Flickr for video (Anyone can upload, anyone can watch)</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td colspan="2" class="lc-unfair">
      <div class="lc-h">Unfair Advantage</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>Network effect of an indexed video catalog pairing viewers and creators; distribution via Google; vast viewer watch-time data and creator fanbase moats</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td rowspan="2" colspan="2" class="lc-customer">
      <div class="lc-h">Customer Segments</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>Viewers seeking on-demand video entertainment and knowledge (Users)</li>
          <li>Video creators and media looking for an audience and distribution (Supply)</li>
          <li>Advertisers looking to buy audience attention (Paying Customers)</li>
        </ul>
        <div class="lc-sub"><b>Early Adopters</b>
          <ul>
          <li>Internet-savvy individuals wanting to share short video clips more easily than via email or message boards</li>
        </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td colspan="2" class="lc-metrics">
      <div class="lc-h">Key Metrics</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>Acquisition: New viewers (including logged-out), new creator signups</li>
          <li>Activation: First video playback completion or first video upload</li>
          <li>Retention: Watch time, repeat visits, sustained creator upload cadence</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td colspan="2" class="lc-channels">
      <div class="lc-h">Channels</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>Embeds on blogs/websites, URL sharing, search, related video recommendations</li>
          <li>Google search integration and ecosystem properties; word-of-mouth</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td colspan="5" class="lc-cost">
      <div class="lc-h">Cost Structure</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>Fixed: Product development, infrastructure, content moderation, general &amp; administrative</li>
          <li>Variable: CDN streaming bandwidth, storage, ad-serving infrastructure, creator revenue payouts</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td colspan="5" class="lc-revenue">
      <div class="lc-h">Revenue Streams</div>
      <div class="lc-b">
        <ul>
          <li>Model: Video advertising (selling viewer attention to advertisers; later Premium subscription)</li>
          <li>Pricing: Ad pricing based on CPM / CPV varies widely by campaign. No flat public price</li>
          <li>LTV: Undecided. No public LTV figure. Derived backwards from advertiser spend and creator inventory</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
